## YouTube demo description

A recall letter arrives. A clinic still has to find the stock, resolve missing labels, and record the response.

Lotlight connects a manufacturer notice and an inventory spreadsheet in one explainable workflow. This demo follows a fictional clinic through source review, exact identifier checks, browser AI, a missing-lot completion gate, and a documented response.

Try the app: https://lotlight-care.web.app

Source code: https://github.com/shi1720/DSH-Hacks-V2

What you will see:
- Importing stock and preserving notice evidence
- AI-assisted product-description comparison
- Manufacturer, catalog, and lot matching
- Human source review and physical-label verification
- Owners, quantities, response evidence, and audit exports

A project by Shivam Gupta for DSH Hacks V2, AI x Healthcare.

This video uses an actual browser recording, AI-generated narration, and captions. The demonstration uses a real historical Baxter notice with a limited two-pair training extract. The clinic, inventory, staff actions, and evidence references are synthetic. It does not show current recall status or real patient data.

AI tools assisted the project's research, design, implementation, testing, and presentation preparation. Product-description similarity is not recall probability. Lotlight is a hackathon decision-support MVP, not a clinically validated or certified recall-management system. Follow complete current manufacturer instructions for real-world actions.

Historical source: https://www.medline.com/media/assets/pdf/vendor-list/FA-2025-039-Customer-Letter-Final-Combined.pdf
