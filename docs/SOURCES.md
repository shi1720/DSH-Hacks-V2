# Evidence and source register

Accessed 16 September 2026. Real sources inform the project; all clinic stock and workflow activity in the demonstration are synthetic.

1. **Baxter manufacturer letter FA-2025-039, 29 August 2025**, hosted by Medline: https://www.medline.com/media/assets/pdf/vendor-list/FA-2025-039-Customer-Letter-Final-Combined.pdf
   - Hero pairs: catalog 2C8632 / lot R25C31031; catalog 2R8858 / lot R25A13024. The demo is a two-pair paraphrased extract, not the whole notice.
   - Only short action wording is quoted. Source routes differ for direct versus distributor purchases. Current full instructions must be verified.
2. **FDA recall database corroboration for 2R8858:** https://www.accessdata.fda.gov/scripts/cdrh/cfdocs/cfres/res.cfm?id=216079
3. **FDA, What is a Medical Device Recall?** https://www.fda.gov/medical-devices/medical-device-recalls-and-early-alerts/what-medical-device-recall
   - Correction and removal differ. The word recall alone does not justify generated stop-use advice.
4. **openFDA device recall documentation:** https://open.fda.gov/apis/device/recall/
   - Weekly research data is not a public-alert or recall-lifecycle source. Lotlight does not use it as such.
5. **ECRI, gaps in home-use device recalls, 2023:** https://home.ecri.org/blogs/ecri-news/gaps-in-recalls-of-home-use-medical-devices-top-ecris-hazards-list-for-2023
   - Evidence of a recognized recall-response problem; not proof of Lotlight's impact or clinic market size.
6. **ECRI competitor category:** https://www.ecri.org/topics/Pages/TopicLanding.aspx?k=%2A&rf=ECRItopics%3Aequals%28%27Hazard+and+Recall+Management%27%29&topicid=d86cb7c83d0d454f8261037d1b22e1d9
   - Established recall-management offerings. No claim to invent the category or outperform ECRI.
7. **MiniLM ONNX model card and weights:** https://huggingface.co/Xenova/all-MiniLM-L6-v2/tree/751bff37182d3f1213fa05d7196b954e230abad9
   - Apache-2.0. Quantized model approximately 23 MB. General sentence embeddings, not a clinically validated recall model.
8. **Transformers.js official repository:** https://github.com/huggingface/transformers.js
9. **DSH Hacks V2 rules:** https://dsh-hacks-v2.devpost.com/rules
   - AI use allowed; participants must be 13+ and members of an educational institution. Submission deadline in the supplied overview: 8 November 2026 at 13:15 IST, equivalent to 7 November 23:45 PST. Recheck official page before submission.
10. **DSH V1 examples:** https://www.dshhacks.org/
   - The organizer highlights lily-memo, Yachay Lab and SciSim. The transferable presentation lesson is a specific problem and visible user action, not imitation of their products.

All business prices, labor rates, pilot targets, and future differentiators in the materials are labeled assumptions or proposals. There are no measured clinical outcomes, customer interviews, signed pilots, or revenue claims.
